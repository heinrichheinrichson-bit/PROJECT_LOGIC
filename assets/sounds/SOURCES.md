# Sound asset sources

`hashi_connect.wav` is a shortened, filtered, volume-adjusted and faded
derivative of an electric sound effect downloaded from Pixabay:

- Source search: https://pixabay.com/sound-effects/search/electric%20dizzle/
- License: Pixabay Content License
- Downloaded: 2026-08-13
- Attribution required: no
- Use: embedded interaction sound in PROJECT_LOGIC; not distributed as a
  standalone sound product

All other WAV files in this directory are generated from scratch by
`tool/generate_sound_assets.dart`.
